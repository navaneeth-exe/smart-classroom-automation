## 2. Interactive Presentation Transport Controls

The bottom presentation dock provides presenters with:
- **`[ ▶ Play Demo ]` / `[ ❚❚ Pause ]`:** Automatically steps through scenarios 1 through 14 with a configurable dwell time (default: 4.5 seconds per step).
- **`[ ◄ Step Prev ]` / `[ Step Next ► ]`:** Enables the presenter to advance or reverse one scenario at a time while answering examiner questions.
- **Interactive Scrubber Bar:** Clickable breadcrumbs for all 14 scenarios allowing instant jumps to specific test conditions (e.g. jumping straight to Step 11 "Total Dismissal / Shutdown").
